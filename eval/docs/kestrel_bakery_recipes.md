# Kestrel Bakery house recipes

## Sourdough loaf

Mix 500 g of strong white flour, 350 g of water, 100 g of active starter and 10 g of
salt. Let the dough rise for 6 hours at room temperature, folding it every hour. Bake in
a covered pot for 45 minutes at 230 degrees Celsius.

## Ginger biscuits

Cream 150 g of butter with 100 g of soft brown sugar, then stir in 250 g of flour,
2 teaspoons of ground ginger and 3 tablespoons of golden syrup. Bake the biscuits for
12 minutes at 170 degrees Celsius. They keep for two weeks in a tin.

## Oat flapjacks

Melt 175 g of butter with 125 g of honey and mix with 300 g of rolled oats. Press into a
tray and bake for 25 minutes at 180 degrees Celsius. Cut into squares while still warm.
